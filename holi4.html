Holi

About Holi

Holi is a popular festival of colors celebrated in India. People celebrate Holi by applying colorful powders and splashing water on each other. On the night before Holi, people gather for **Holika Dahan**, where a bonfire is lit. Families and friends come together, share sweets, and enjoy delicious food. Holi is a festival of joy, friendship, love, and togetherness.
