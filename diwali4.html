Diwali Festival

About Diwali

Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, is one of the most popular festivals celebrated in India. It symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. People celebrate Diwali by cleaning and decorating their homes, lighting diyas and candles, wearing new clothes, preparing delicious sweets, and spending time with family and friends. Many people also perform Lakshmi Puja and enjoy fireworks. Diwali brings happiness, peace, and togetherness, making it a joyful and special festival for everyone.
