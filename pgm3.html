About Us

MEA Engineering College is a leading self-financing engineering college in Malappuram district,located at vengoor near Perinthalmanna. The college is committed to providing quality and value-based education with ethics and professionalism. Its offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in engineering ,technology and computer applications. The college is approved by AICTE and affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University

Course offered

All course offered by RIT are affiliated to kerela Technological University and approved by the ALL Indian Council for Technical Education(AICTE). The B.Tech programmes are of 8 semester(4 years) duration. The B.Arch programme is of 10 semesters(5 years) duration. The M.tech programmes are 4 semester(2 years) duration. MCA programme is of 4 semester(2 years) duration.
