Vegetables are an important part of a healthy diet. They are rich in vitamins, minerals, fiber, and other nutrients that help keep our body strong and healthy. Vegetables such as carrots, potatoes, spinach, tomatoes, cabbage, and beans are nutritious and delicious. Eating different kinds of vegetables regularly helps improve digestion, strengthens the immune system, and keeps us active. We should include fresh vegetables in our daily meals to stay healthy and fit.
