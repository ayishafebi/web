Fruits are an important part of a healthy and balanced diet. They are rich in vitamins, minerals, fiber, and antioxidants that help keep our body strong and healthy. Fruits such as apples, bananas, oranges, mangoes, grapes, and watermelons are delicious and provide many health benefits. Eating a variety of fresh fruits regularly can improve digestion, support the immune system, and provide energy for daily activities. Fruits are also colorful and tasty, making them a wonderful addition to our meals.
