Onam

About Onam

Onam is a popular harvest festival celebrated mainly in Kerala. It is a joyful festival that brings people together regardless of their religion or background. Onam celebrates the return of the legendary King Mahabali, whose reign is remembered as a time of peace and prosperity. People decorate their homes with beautiful **pookalam** (flower designs), wear traditional clothes, prepare a grand feast called **Onam Sadya**, and enjoy traditional games and cultural programs. Onam is a festival of happiness, unity, prosperity, and togetherness.
